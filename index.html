Welcome to EvenchinSMP! By joining the server, you agree to follow these rules. Not knowing the rules does not excuse breaking them. If you're unsure whether a mod is allowed, ask staff before using it.

01.General Rules

  1. Treat all players with respect.
  2. No harassment, bullying or targeted insults.
  3. No racism, discrimination or hate speech.
  4. No spam or excessive caps in chat.
  5. No advertising other servers without permission.
  6. Do not impersonate staff members.
  7. Do not abuse bugs or glitches.
  8. Follow reasonable instructions given by staff.
  9. Do not evade punishments using alternate accounts.
  10. Help keep the server friendly and enjoyable.

02.Forbidden Mods & Clients BANNED

Any client, mod or external tool that automates gameplay or gives an unfair advantage is prohibited. Examples include:

Combat cheats

KillAura, AutoAttack, Aim Assist, TriggerBot and automated clicking.

Movement cheats

Fly, Speed, NoFall, AntiKnockback, Reach and Scaffold hacks.

Automation

Baritone, automated mining, automated farming and gameplay bots.

Hacked clients

Wurst, Meteor, LiquidBounce and other clients when used with prohibited features.

Freecam is prohibited when used to scout, access or gain information that would normally be unavailable to your player.

03.Forbidden Visual Modifications BANNED

The following visual modifications are not allowed:

  • X-Ray texture packs and X-Ray mods.
  • Ore highlighting or overlays that reveal hidden ores.
  • Player or entity ESP and radar.
  • Cave finders and tools that expose hidden underground areas.
  • Transparent-block packs designed to reveal hidden resources.
  • Fullbright modifications if they provide a visibility advantage beyond server rules.
  • Resource packs that reveal information normally hidden by the game.

Visual changes must not reveal hidden information or provide an unfair advantage.

04.Generally Allowed Mods ALLOWED

These are generally permitted when used only for their ordinary, non-cheating functions:

  • Sodium, Lithium and FerriteCore (performance).
  • Mod Menu.
  • AppleSkin.
  • ShulkerBoxTooltip.
  • Replay Mod (for recording and reviewing gameplay).
  • Cosmetic resource packs and ordinary visual customization.
  • Shaders, provided they do not reveal hidden information or bypass server restrictions.

Important: A mod's name alone does not guarantee it is allowed. Features that provide an unfair advantage remain prohibited. Ask staff if you're unsure.

05.Exploiting & Cheating

  1. Duplication glitches are prohibited unless staff explicitly announces otherwise.
  2. Do not exploit server bugs for personal gain.
  3. Do not use exploits to access restricted areas.
  4. Do not abuse unintended game mechanics to gain an unfair advantage.
  5. Report serious bugs to the staff team.

06.PvP Rules

  1. No combat hacks or unfair combat automation.
  2. No exploiting PvP glitches.
  3. Respect agreed PvP arrangements and event rules.
  4. Do not use prohibited modifications to gain an advantage.

07.Punishments

Depending on the severity and frequency of a violation, staff may issue:

  • Warning
  • Temporary mute
  • Temporary ban
  • Permanent ban

Severe violations may result in an immediate ban. Staff will consider the situation and available evidence when deciding on a punishment.

08.Final Notice

Playing on EvenchinSMP means agreeing to these rules. Rules may be updated when needed, so check this page for changes.

Have fun, play fair and respect everyone. Welcome to EvenchinSMP!
